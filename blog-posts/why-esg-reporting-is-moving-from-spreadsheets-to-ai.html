Why ESG reporting is moving from spreadsheets to AI

For years, sustainability reporting lived in spreadsheets: one tab per site, one email chain per data request, and a last-minute scramble before every deadline. That approach worked when disclosure was voluntary. It struggles now that reporting is mandatory for a growing number of companies and their suppliers.

Where spreadsheets break down

  • Data arrives in different formats from different owners, so every cycle starts with cleaning.
  • Errors are hard to spot and harder to trace back to a source.
  • Mapping the same data to several frameworks means re-entering it each time.
  • Auditors need evidence, and a spreadsheet rarely records who changed what.

What AI changes

AI-assisted workflows validate data as it arrives, flag gaps and anomalies early, and draft disclosure commentary from the underlying numbers for a person to review. Every figure can keep a link to its source, owner and approval history, which is what assurance providers look for.

Keep people in charge
