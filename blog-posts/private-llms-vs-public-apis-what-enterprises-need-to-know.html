Private LLMs vs public APIs: what enterprises actually need to know

Public AI services make it easy to start, but many organisations cannot send confidential, regulated or proprietary data outside their own boundary. The choice between a public API and a privately deployed model comes down to control.

Questions to ask

  • Where does our data go, and who can access it?
  • Can we keep the model inside our own cloud tenancy or on-premise?
  • What logging, permissions and guardrails do we need for compliance?
  • How will we monitor quality and cost once it is in production?

When a private deployment makes sense

Private deployment fits regulated industries, sensitive financial or ESG data, and internal knowledge assistants that must answer from your own documents with citations. It trades some convenience for full control over data, access, cost and compliance.
