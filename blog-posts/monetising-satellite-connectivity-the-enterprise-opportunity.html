Monetising satellite connectivity: the enterprise opportunity

Satellite broadband is reaching enterprises, remote operations and communities that terrestrial networks cannot serve. For operators, ISPs and integrators, the opportunity is real, but hardware alone does not create revenue.

What it takes to capture value

  • Service packaging: plans, bundles and enterprise offers for different segments.
  • Customer and subscription management for terminals and accounts.
  • Usage metering, invoicing and reconciliation.
  • Support, partner and reseller management.
