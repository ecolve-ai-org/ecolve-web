How AI is changing the CFO’s monthly close

The monthly close is still a manual race in many finance teams: pull the numbers, reconcile them, explain what moved and assemble the board pack. Much of that effort is repetitive, which makes it a good fit for AI.

Where AI helps

  • Generating management accounts and board packs directly from finance data.
  • Identifying the variances that matter and drafting plain-English commentary on the drivers.
  • Monitoring KPIs continuously and alerting when a metric moves outside its expected range.
  • Answering questions in natural language, grounded in your own numbers.

Trust and traceability

Finance teams need to know where a number came from. Good tooling keeps a transparent trail from every statement back to source data, and lets people review before anything is published.
