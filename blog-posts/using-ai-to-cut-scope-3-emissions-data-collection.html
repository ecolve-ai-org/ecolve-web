Using AI to cut Scope 3 emissions data collection from weeks to days

Scope 3 emissions usually make up the largest share of a company’s footprint and are the hardest to measure, because the data sits with suppliers and partners rather than inside your own systems.

Why collection takes so long

  • Suppliers use different formats and levels of detail.
  • Gaps and inconsistencies are found late, after the data has been chased.
  • Follow-ups are manual and easy to lose track of.

How AI helps

Streamlined supplier engagement, automated validation and gap detection catch problems as data arrives instead of at the end of the cycle. Teams spend less time chasing and more time acting on the results.
