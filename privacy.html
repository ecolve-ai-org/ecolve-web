Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ecolve.ai (“ecolve.ai”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you visit our website at ecolve.ai (the “Site”) or otherwise interact with us. By using the Site, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Site.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information we need to run the Site and provide our services.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill in a contact or enquiry form, subscribe to updates, book a demo or email us, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, email address, company name, job title and the content of your message.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the Site, our hosting provider and our systems may automatically record basic technical information in server logs, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages requested, and the date and time of your visit. We use this information to keep the Site secure and working properly.

Cookies. The Site currently uses only cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. See Section 5 for details.

Information from third parties. If you contact us through a third-party platform (for example, LinkedIn or a scheduling tool), we may receive the information you share through that platform.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information (such as health data, government identifiers or financial account details) through the Site. Please do not send us such information.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide the information or services you request
  • Operate, maintain, secure and improve the Site and our services
  • Send you updates, news or marketing communications, where you have asked for them or where permitted by law (you can opt out at any time)
  • Detect, prevent and address fraud, abuse, security incidents and technical problems
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

4. Legal bases for processing

Where data protection laws such as the EU/UK GDPR apply, we process personal information on the following bases: your consent (for example, non-essential cookies and marketing emails); performance of a contract or steps taken at your request before entering into one; our legitimate interests (such as securing and improving the Site and communicating with prospective customers), balanced against your rights; and compliance with legal obligations.

5. Cookies and analytics

The Site does not currently use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use third-party analytics tools. We may use cookies that are strictly necessary for the Site to function, for example to keep the Site secure or to remember your cookie choices.

If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first. You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

6. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Microsoft 365 (Microsoft Corporation), which provides our email, so messages you send us are stored and processed there
  • Hostinger, which hosts the Site and may process technical data such as IP addresses in its server logs
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers, accountants and insurers, where needed
  • Authorities or other parties where required by law, legal process or to protect rights, safety and security
  • A successor in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case we will ensure the information remains protected

7. Our products and customer deployments

This policy covers the ecolve.ai website and our direct dealings with visitors and prospective customers. It does not cover data processed inside ecolve.ai products after they are deployed for a customer.

We deploy our products in the customer’s own cloud tenant or on the customer’s premises. In those deployments the customer controls the environment and the data in it, and the customer’s own privacy notices and its agreement with us govern how that data is handled. [To confirm: ecolve.ai does not receive or store customer data outside the customer’s environment, except where the customer gives us access, for example for support.]

Where we have access to customer data to provide support or maintenance, we handle it only on the customer’s instructions and as set out in our agreement with them. [To confirm: we do not use customer data to train AI models unless the customer agrees in writing.]

If you are an individual whose data is processed by one of our customers using our products, please contact that customer about your rights.

8. International transfers

Our service providers, such as Microsoft and Hostinger, may be located in countries other than your own, including countries whose data protection laws differ from yours. Where we transfer personal information across borders, we take steps to ensure it remains protected, such as using contractual safeguards or relying on recognized transfer mechanisms where required.

9. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including to meet legal, accounting or reporting requirements. For example, enquiry details are generally kept while we are in contact with you and for a reasonable period afterwards. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

10. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive your information in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing
  • Opt out of marketing communications (use the unsubscribe link in any email)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

These rights may be provided under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, and Sri Lanka’s Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022. To exercise any of them, email us at the address in Section 15. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

12. Children’s privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Links to other sites

The Site may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top. If changes are material, we will take reasonable steps to notify you, for example by posting a notice on the Site.
